## What is a candlestick

A **candlestick** shows how the price moved during one period, for example one hour. It has
four values: _open_, _high_, _low_ and _close_. Read more in the
[Bitcoin whitepaper](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf).

### Parts of a candle

1. The **body** – between the open and the close price.
2. The **wicks** – the highest and the lowest price.
3. The **color** – green when the price went up, red when it went down.

- Short body: buyers and sellers were balanced.
- Long body: one side was clearly stronger.
  - Long upper wick: the price was pushed back down.
  - Long lower wick: the price was pushed back up.

> The trend is your friend – until it ends.

## Order types

| Order  | When it fills                    | Price                |
| ------ | -------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Market | immediately                      | best available       |
| Limit  | when the price reaches the limit | your price or better |
| Stop   | after the stop price is hit      | market               |

~~Buy high, sell low.~~ Plan your exit before you enter.

## Placing an order

An order is an instruction for the exchange. The simplest one is a <KeyTerm term="market-order">market order</KeyTerm>. In code it looks like `buy(0.01, "BTC")`:

```ts
const order = { side: "buy", amount: 0.01, symbol: "BTC/USDT" };
```
