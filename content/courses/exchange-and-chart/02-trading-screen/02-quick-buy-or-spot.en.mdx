Almost every exchange has two entrances. The main entrance, labelled **Buy crypto**, is big, colourful and
simple. The back entrance, labelled **Spot**, leads straight to the market – it looks more complicated, but
you usually pay less for the same bitcoin there.

<Illustration name="quickBuyMarkup" />

## Quick buy

Buttons such as **Buy crypto**, **Quick buy** or **Convert** work like a currency exchange office: you
enter an amount, the exchange shows what you will get, done. But the price is usually not the market
price. The exchange hides a markup in it – the so-called <KeyTerm term="spread">spread</KeyTerm> – and
sometimes adds a fee on top.

## The spot market

On the **Spot** tab you trade directly with others through the order book. You pay a visible trading fee,
usually tenths of a percent for ordinary customers. More in lesson 8.

## What it adds up to

A 400 € purchase (sample numbers):

| Method                                       | Cost (roughly) |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| quick buy with a 1.5% markup                 | 6 €            |
| quick buy by card: 1.5% markup + 2% card fee | 14 €           |
| spot with a 0.2% fee                         | 0.80 €         |

_Exact numbers differ by exchange and amount – you will find them in the price list and the trade preview._

And note: you pay **both when buying and when selling**. Whoever buys and sells through quick buy can
easily lose 3% – without the price moving at all.

## When convenience is worth it

- **A small one-off amount**, where the difference is pocket change and you do not want to learn a new
  panel.
- **A first purchase**, when you just want to see how it works.

For regular purchases and larger amounts, spot pays off. Check the recurring buy (DCA) that exchanges
offer before turning it on, too – it often goes through the same expensive quick buy.

<Callout type="warning" title="Compare the price, not the fee">
  Quick buy often tempts with a "zero fee". But a markup in the price is a fee
  too, just less visible. Compare how much crypto you get for the same amount.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"Quick buy has no fee, so it is the cheapest."** The fee is hidden in the price.
- **"Spot is only for professionals."** It is the same purchase, with one more field.
- **"A few percent does not matter."** It adds up on every purchase and sale.

<Callout title="Summary">

- Quick buy is convenient but usually has a markup in the price and sometimes a fee.
- On spot you pay a visible fee, usually tenths of a percent for ordinary customers.
- You pay costs on both buying and selling – with quick buy that easily adds up to several percent.
- Quick buy for small tries, spot for regular and larger purchases.

</Callout>
