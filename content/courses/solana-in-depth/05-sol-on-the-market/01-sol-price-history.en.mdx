## The chapters

| Period        | What happened                                               | Price (daily extremes)                 |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 2020–2021     | exchange listing and a huge bull market                     | from 1.03 USD (Dec 2020) to 259.90 USD |
| Nov 2021–2022 | bear market, the FTX collapse                               | a 97% fall to 8 USD                    |
| 2023–2024     | comeback, memecoins, record network activity                | from 8 USD to 264 USD                  |
| January 2025  | the TRUMP token frenzy                                      | a record 295.83 USD (19 Jan)           |
| 2025–2026     | the market cooled; first US spot SOL ETFs from October 2025 | an 80% fall to 60.13 USD               |

End of September 2026: 118 USD.

## What follows from it

- **Drops of 80–97% are not an exception but a pattern.** SOL went through them twice in six years. Many
  tokens from 2021, though, never recovered from a similar fall – SOL's survival is no rule for others.
- **Good news does not guarantee a rise.** The ETF launch in October 2025 was a big event – and SOL then
  fell to a third.
- **The purchase date matters more than people admit.** Whoever bought at 260 USD in November 2021 was
  still at a loss in September 2026 – five years later.
- **The logarithmic scale.** On an ordinary chart a rise from 3 to 30 USD would look like a flat line and a
  fall from 260 to 130 like a disaster. Yet the first is +900% and the second −50%.

<Callout type="risk" title="The past need not repeat">
  The fact that SOL has come back from deep drops before does not mean it will
  again. History shows how big the moves can be – not where the price will go.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"SOL always comes back."** So far it has. That describes the past, not a guarantee.
- **"After an 80% fall it cannot fall further."** In 2022 it fell 97% – from any price it can fall another
  50%.
- **"ETF = a rise."** After the ETF launch SOL fell to a third.

<Callout title="Summary">

- SOL rose from under 3 USD to 260, fell 97% to 8 USD, returned to a record 295.83 and fell 80% again.
- Drops of 80% or more are a repeated experience for SOL, not an exception.
- Neither good news (ETFs) nor famous names (TRUMP) guarantee a rise.
- Read charts with big moves on a logarithmic scale.

</Callout>
