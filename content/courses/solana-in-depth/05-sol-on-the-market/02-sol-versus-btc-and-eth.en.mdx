SOL has its own story – a fast network, memecoins, ETFs. On a chart, though, it mostly behaves like a dog
on a leash: where Bitcoin goes, SOL goes too. It just jumps around more, sometimes runs ahead and
sometimes lets itself be dragged.

## Year by year

Daily Binance data from January 2021 to September 2026:

| Year          | Correlation with BTC | SOL volatility | BTC volatility | Beta | SOL return | BTC return |
| ------------- | -------------------- | -------------- | -------------- | ---- | ---------- | ---------- |
| 2021          | 0.40                 | 162%           | 81%            | 0.79 | +11,167%   | +60%       |
| 2022          | 0.79                 | 117%           | 64%            | 1.46 | −94%       | −64%       |
| 2023          | 0.61                 | 99%            | 44%            | 1.38 | +920%      | +156%      |
| 2024          | 0.74                 | 82%            | 53%            | 1.15 | +86%       | +121%      |
| 2025          | 0.80                 | 86%            | 42%            | 1.65 | −34%       | −6%        |
| 2026 (to Sep) | 0.88                 | 64%            | 46%            | 1.22 | −5%        | −5%        |

Volatility is annual, calculated from daily changes. Over the whole period: a correlation of 0.62, SOL
volatility 109% against 57% for Bitcoin.

What you read from it:

- **Correlation is rising.** In 2021 SOL had a life of its own (0.40); in 2026 it moved with Bitcoin more
  than ever (0.88). Similarly with Ether – 0.88 in 2026.
- **SOL swings about twice as much.** On 147 days it moved more than 10% in a day, Bitcoin on only 24.
- **It does not protect in downturns.** Of the 87 days when Bitcoin fell more than 5%, SOL fell on 79 – by
  8.3% on average against 7.3% for Bitcoin.

## Beta: the amplifier

The so-called <KeyTerm term="beta">beta</KeyTerm> tells you how much an asset moves on average when the
market moves 1%. SOL's beta to Bitcoin came out at 1.18 over the whole period, and as high as 1.65 in
2025: when Bitcoin fell 1%, SOL fell 1.65% on average. But beta changes from year to year – in 2021 it was
below one.

## The SOL/BTC ratio

If you want to know whether SOL did **better than Bitcoin**, look at the SOL/BTC price ratio. It rises
when SOL leads and falls when it lags.

From January 2023 to March 2024 SOL rose more than fourfold against Bitcoin (index 428). Then the trend
turned, and in May 2026 the index stood at 156 – SOL had lost more than 60% against Bitcoin from its peak.
Even when SOL set a dollar record in January 2025, it was already about a fifth below its peak against Bitcoin.

<Callout type="warning" title="An altcoin is not diversification">
  SOL next to Bitcoin in a portfolio does not spread the risk – it adds a bet on
  the same market with roughly double the swings. In downturns it falls with
  Bitcoin, and usually more.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"SOL has its own fundamentals, it is unrelated to Bitcoin."** A correlation of 0.6–0.9 in most years says otherwise.
- **"SOL went up, so it is doing well."** It may have lost against Bitcoin – look at the SOL/BTC ratio.
- **"Beta is a fixed number."** It changes from year to year, from 0.79 to 1.65.

<Callout title="Summary">

- SOL moves ever more closely with Bitcoin: correlation from 0.40 in 2021 to 0.88 in 2026.
- It swings about twice as much and usually falls more than Bitcoin in downturns.
- Beta (1.18 over the whole period) shows how SOL amplifies Bitcoin's moves; it changes over time.
- The SOL/BTC ratio shows whether SOL beats Bitcoin; since March 2024 it has lost ground.

</Callout>
