Congratulations – you now know more about Solana than most people who trade memecoins on it. You know how

## What to take away from the course

- **The network:** fast and cheap thanks to powerful hardware, a leader schedule and Proof of History. It
  pays for that with fewer validators and a history of outages.
- **SOL:** no fixed cap, falling inflation; staking mainly protects your share from dilution.
- **The wallet:** everything is an account, tokens have their own accounts with a refundable deposit, and
  one signature can approve many instructions at once.
- **Staking:** native staking is simple and your SOL stays yours; liquid staking adds liquidity and risks.
- **DeFi and memecoins:** the price in a pool depends on liquidity, slippage invites bots and the vast
  majority of memecoins die.
- **The market:** SOL swings about twice as much as Bitcoin, moves with it and has already fallen 80% or
  more twice.

## Ten rules

1. Never type your seed phrase anywhere unless you need to restore the wallet.
2. Keep savings in a hardware wallet and experiments in a separate wallet.
3. Read the transaction preview before signing. If you do not understand it, do not sign.
4. Ignore unsolicited tokens and NFTs and do not click their links.
5. You recognise a token by its mint address, not by its name and logo.
6. For an unknown token, check the authorities, holders and liquidity – and try selling a small amount.
7. Keep slippage low and watch price impact on big trades.
8. Keep a little SOL in your account for fees.
9. Choose a validator by commission and reliability and watch whether it raises its commission.
10. Put into memecoins only money you consider spent.
