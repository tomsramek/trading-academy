A moving average shows the direction. Momentum indicators ask something else: **how strong is the
move?** The best known of them is <KeyTerm term="rsi">RSI</KeyTerm> (Relative Strength Index),
introduced by J. Welles Wilder in 1978.

## What RSI measures

RSI compares the average rises and the average falls over the last 14 periods and converts the result
to a scale of 0 to 100:

- **around 50** – rises and falls are balanced,
- **above 70** – rises clearly dominate, the market is so-called <KeyTerm term="overbought">overbought</KeyTerm>,
- **below 30** – falls dominate, the market is oversold.

It sounds simple: sell above 70, buy below 30. And that is exactly the most expensive beginner's
mistake.

<Illustration name="speedometer" />

## Overbought does not mean sell

Look at Bitcoin in autumn 2020. From mid-October to 25 November RSI was above 70 – almost six weeks in
a row. Anyone selling "because the market is overbought" sold at 11,500, at 13,000, at 15,000… The
price meanwhile rose from 11,503 to 18,719. And a month later it was above 24,000.

A high RSI in a strong trend does not mean the move is ending. It means it is **strong**. An
overbought market can become even more overbought.

## How to use RSI sensibly

- **As a strength thermometer, not a trigger.** RSI above 50 and rising confirms that buyers are in
  control.
- **It works better in a range than in a trend.** When the price stays between support and
  resistance, RSI near the edges of the range can help with timing. In a strong trend it will mislead
  you.
- **Divergence as a warning.** When the price makes a new high but RSI does not, the move is losing
  strength. Such a warning can take weeks to play out, though – and may never play out at all.

<Callout type="warning" title="The indicator is calculated from the price">
  RSI sees nothing that is not already in the price. It brings no new
  information – it only shows the information you already have in a different
  way. Never decide on it alone.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"RSI 80 – it has to fall."** In January 2021 Bitcoin's RSI was around 90 and the price was above
  40,000.
- **"RSI below 30 is a sure buy."** In a crash it can stay below 30 as long as above 70 in a rally.
- **"I will change the setting to 7 and it will be more accurate."** It will be faster – and with more
  false signals.

<Callout title="Summary">

- RSI measures the strength of a move on a 0–100 scale; the 70 and 30 lines mark overbought and
  oversold.
- Overbought does not mean sell – in a strong trend RSI stays high for weeks.
- It works best as a confirmation of strength and in a range; divergence is only a warning.
- RSI is calculated from the price and brings no new information.

</Callout>
