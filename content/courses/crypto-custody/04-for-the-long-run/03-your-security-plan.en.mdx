Congratulations – you have finished the Keeping crypto safe course. You now know more than most people who
hold crypto: who has the keys, how a hardware wallet works, how to back up and how to think of your
family. All that is left is to do it. Here is a plan for one afternoon and a few years of peace.

<Illustration name="securityShield" />

## Now

- Decide what to leave on an exchange and what to take with you.
- Turn on 2FA on your exchange via an app or a security key, not via SMS.
- Set a PIN with your mobile operator for changes to your number.
- Check that you do not have your seed phrase anywhere as a photo, file or note. If you do, create a
  new wallet and move the money.

## Within a month

- Buy a hardware wallet directly from the manufacturer or its authorised reseller.
- Set it up: a new seed phrase on the device, words copied by hand, a PIN, current firmware.
- Test a recovery with a small amount, and only then move your savings.
- Make a second backup (ideally metal) and store it in a different place.
- Write a letter for your loved ones: what you own, where the backups are, whom to contact – without
  the seed phrase.

## Once a year

- Check that the backups are legible, complete and in their place.
- Update the letter for your loved ones.
- Update the firmware and the app from official sources.
- Review and revoke permissions to spend tokens you no longer need.

## Rules that always apply

1. No honest party ever asks for your seed phrase – ever.
2. Check the address and amount on the device's screen, not on the monitor.
3. Do not sign anything you do not understand.
4. Send a test amount to a new recipient first.
5. Do not brag.

<Callout type="risk" title="The most important thing, last">
  Keeping crypto safe protects against losing keys and theft, not against
  falling prices. Crypto can lose much of its value even in a perfectly secured
  wallet. Invest only money you can afford to lose.
</Callout>
