- find out what a forward test is and why a backtest is not enough
- learn to move to real money step by step

</Callout>

A backtest and walk-forward work with a past you already know. The final exam is the future: trading by
the rules in real time, with no way to "peek ahead". This is called a <KeyTerm term="forward-test">forward test</KeyTerm>.

<Illustration name="testPilot" />

## 1. A forward test without money

For a few months, trade by the rules on a demo account or just in your journal. Record every signal right
when it appears – not afterwards. You are checking two things:

- **Do the results match the backtest?** Not exactly – but they should be in a similar range (win rate,
  average win and loss, drawdown).
- **Can you follow the rules?** In a backtest nobody hesitates. In reality people do.

## 2. Small money

If the forward test shows nothing worrying, move to **small amounts** – so small that a loss does not hurt.
Real money brings what a demo does not: slippage, outages, withdrawal fees and above all emotions.

## 3. Scaling up step by step

Increase the size **in steps and by a rule**, not by mood. For example: after every 30 trades, if the
results are within the backtest range, raise the risk per trade by a quarter – at most to the level in your
trading plan.

## The switch-off rule

You make the most important decision **in advance**: when you will switch the strategy off. For example:

- the drawdown exceeds **1.5 times** the maximum drawdown in the backtest,
- a losing streak is **longer than the longest streak** in the backtest,
- the market changes so fundamentally that the hypothesis no longer holds.

Without such a rule, in a bad period you will think "just a bit longer" – and "just a bit longer" can last a
long time and cost a lot.

<Callout type="risk" title="A successful test is not a guarantee">
  Even a strategy that passed a backtest, walk-forward and a forward test may
  not make money in the future. Tests only lower the chance that you are doing
  something obviously wrong. Risk only money you can afford to lose.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"The backtest worked, I am going straight in with the whole account."** You skipped the two cheapest
  steps.
- **"A demo is not real, it is pointless."** It shows whether you can follow the rules and reveals
  mistakes in them.
- **"I will decide when to switch it off when the time comes."** When the time comes, emotions will decide.

<Callout title="Summary">

- Forward test = trading by the rules in real time, first without money.
- Then small amounts and scaling up step by step, by a rule.
- Set the switch-off rule in advance – based on the drawdown and losing streaks from the backtest.
- Even successful tests do not guarantee a profit.

</Callout>
