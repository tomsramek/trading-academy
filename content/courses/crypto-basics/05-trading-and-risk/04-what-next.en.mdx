## What to take away from the course

- **A blockchain** is a shared record of payments that nobody can quietly rewrite.
- **Bitcoin** has a limited supply, **Ethereum** runs programs; most altcoins and memecoins do not
  survive.
- Choose an **exchange** by its licence, not its ads; never give anyone your **seed phrase**.
- **Scams** share the same signs: a sure profit, urgency and a request for money or details.
- For most people, **investing** makes more sense than trading; if you trade, use a **stop-loss** and
  small risk.

## How to continue safely

1. **Practise without money.** Many platforms offer a demo account where you trade with virtual
   money. Try orders, stop-losses and position sizing without risk.
2. **Keep a journal.** For every trade, write down why, where the stop-loss is and how it turned out.
   In a few months you will see your own mistakes in black and white.
3. **Start with small amounts.** Real money brings emotions a demo never will. Keep it small at
   first.
4. **Have a plan and stick to it.** How much you invest, how often and when you sell – decide in
   advance.
5. **Keep learning** – and be suspicious of anyone who promises a sure profit.
