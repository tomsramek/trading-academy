Prices do not move in straight lines. Some levels stop the price again and again – because many
traders remember them and act there.

## A real example

In the first half of 2024 Bitcoin rose sharply, then spent months between two levels.

- **Resistance** – a level where selling pushed the price back down (around 73,000).
- **Support** – a level where buying pushed it back up (around 56,500).

<Callout type="risk">
  Levels are zones, not exact prices. A level that held three times can break on
  the fourth. Always decide in advance where you are wrong.
</Callout>
