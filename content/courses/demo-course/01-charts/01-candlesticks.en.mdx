A candlestick chart is the most common way to show how a price moved. Each candle covers one
period – a minute, an hour or a day – and packs four prices into one shape.

## Parts of a candle

<CandleAnatomy />

The **body** shows where the price opened and closed. The **wicks** show how far it reached in
between. A green body means the price closed higher than it opened, a red body the opposite.

<Callout>
  Always check the period of the chart first. A long red candle on a 1-minute
  chart means much less than the same candle on a daily chart.
</Callout>
