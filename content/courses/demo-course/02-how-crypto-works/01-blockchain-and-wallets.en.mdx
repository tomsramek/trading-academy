## Keys and addresses

Your coins are not stored in the wallet app – they are recorded on the blockchain. The wallet
keeps the **key** that can move them.

<WalletKeys />

<Callout type="warning" title="Not your keys, not your coins">
  On an exchange, the exchange holds the keys for you. If it fails or freezes
  withdrawals, you cannot move your coins.
</Callout>
