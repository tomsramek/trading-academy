An exchange matches people who want to buy with people who want to sell. All waiting offers
are listed in the **order book**.

<OrderBook />

## Order types

| Order  | When it fills                    | Price                |
| ------ | -------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Market | immediately                      | best available       |
| Limit  | when the price reaches the limit | your price or better |
| Stop   | after the stop price is hit      | market               |

A <KeyTerm term="market-order">market order</KeyTerm> takes the best offers from the book right
away. If there is little waiting, a big order can fill at worse prices – this is called
**slippage**.

<Callout>Use a limit order when the price matters more than speed.</Callout>
