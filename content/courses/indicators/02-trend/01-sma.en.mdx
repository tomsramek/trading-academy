The simple moving average is the oldest and humblest indicator in the menu. It has no opinions and plays
no favourites – every candle in the window gets exactly the same vote. Professor Wick likes it for that:
it is the most democratic line on the chart.

<Illustration name="slidingWindow" />

## How it is computed

The <KeyTerm term="moving-average">moving average</KeyTerm> (SMA, on Binance simply **MA**) adds up the
closing prices of the last N candles and divides by N. With every new candle the window moves: the
oldest price drops out, the newest comes in. Click **Next** and watch a window of 7 candles:

<IndicatorSteps
  data={btc}
  indicator="sma"
  period={7}
  from="2024-03-01"
  label="Computing a simple moving average of 7 candles on the daily Bitcoin chart from March 2024, step by step"
/>

Notice that every candle in the window weighs **the same** – a week-old one as much as yesterday's.
When a big move drops out of the window, the average jumps even though nothing happened today. This is
called the drop-off effect.

## What the period does

The period is the number of candles in the window. A short one keeps the line near the price, a long one
smooths it. Move the period and watch how often the price crosses the average:

<IndicatorPeriod
  data={btc}
  indicator="sma"
  min={3}
  max={200}
  value={25}
  label="Daily Bitcoin chart from October 2023 to December 2024 with a simple moving average whose period you change with a slider"
/>

On Binance three averages are on when you open the chart: **MA(7), MA(25) and MA(99)**. Long-term
traders often use 50 and 200 days – partly because everyone else watches them.

## Setting it on Binance

row. Each of up to six lines has its own length, price source (usually Close), style and colour:

## Golden cross and death cross

When the 50-day average crosses the 200-day average from below, it is called a <KeyTerm term="golden-cross">golden cross</KeyTerm>. The opposite crossing is a death cross. The
2022–2024 chart has three:

<Callout type="warning" title="A golden cross is not an order to buy">
  A golden cross only says that the average price of the last 50 days is above
  the average of the last 200 days. It says nothing about tomorrow. In 2023 the
  first golden cross was followed within seven months by a death cross at a
  price only 11% higher.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"The 200-day average is support the price will bounce off."** Sometimes it does, sometimes it goes through it like a knife through butter.
- **"Golden cross = the start of a bull market."** It is a late confirmation, not a forecast – and in a sideways market they come one after another.
- **"The more averages, the better."** Three averages with similar periods show almost the same thing.

<Callout title="Summary">

- SMA = the sum of the last N closes divided by N; every candle in the window weighs the same.
- A short period keeps the line near the price, a long one smooths and delays it.
- On Binance: Original → indicator icon → Main Indicator → MA (default 7, 25, 99).
- Golden and death crosses are late confirmations, not signals about the future.

</Callout>
