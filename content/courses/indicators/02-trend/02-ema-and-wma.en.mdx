The simple average gives every candle the same vote. But yesterday usually matters to you more than last
month. EMA and WMA therefore give new candles more weight. They are like a friend who mostly remembers
the latest gossip – quick to know what is going on, but sometimes panicking for nothing.

## EMA: a bit of the way every day

The <KeyTerm term="ema">EMA</KeyTerm> (exponential average) is not computed from a window but from its
previous value. Every day it takes a step towards the new close – exactly **2 / (N + 1)** of the way:

- EMA 7: a step of 2 / 8 = **0.25** – a quarter of the way to the new price,
- EMA 25: a step of 2 / 26 ≈ **0.077**,
- EMA 99: a step of 2 / 100 = **0.02**.

Older candles never drop out of the calculation completely; their influence just fades fast. The first
EMA value is a plain average of the first N candles. Go through it:

## WMA: weights like stairs

The <KeyTerm term="wma">WMA</KeyTerm> (weighted average) is computed from a window like the SMA, but the
candles in it have weights like stairs: the newest N, the one before N − 1 … the oldest 1. For WMA 7 the
weights add up to 28, so the newest candle makes 7 / 28 = 25% of the result and the oldest only 1 / 28 ≈
3.6%. Nothing outside the window has any influence.

<Callout type="warning" title="Faster does not mean more accurate">
  EMA and WMA know nothing more about the market than SMA. They just split the
  weight differently among the same past prices. The faster reaction is paid for
  with more false signals – especially in a market without a trend.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"EMA predicts a turn."** EMA only reacts to a turn sooner – after it, not before it.
- **"EMA 25 and SMA 25 are completely different indicators."** Most of the time they run close together; they differ mainly at turns.
- **"WMA is the same as EMA."** WMA forgets everything outside its window; EMA remembers the whole history with fading weight.

<Callout title="Summary">

- EMA takes a step of 2 / (N + 1) of the way to the new price every day; older candles never fully drop out.
- WMA weighs the candles in its window N, N − 1 … 1; nothing outside the window counts.
- Both react sooner than SMA – to real and to false turns.
- On Binance: Original → indicator icon → Main Indicator → EMA and WMA (default 7, 25, 99).

</Callout>
