Supertrend is a handrail on a staircase: in a rise it runs below the price and only moves up, never
down. As long as the price holds on to it, you can keep climbing. When the price drops through it, the
handrail moves above it. Professor Wick says it is the most honest line in the menu – it never backs
down.

## ATR: how big a normal move is

Before we get to Supertrend we need its engine – <KeyTerm term="atr">ATR</KeyTerm> (average true range).
A candle's true range is the **largest** of three numbers:

- the candle's high − low,
- the jump up from yesterday's close (high − yesterday's close),
- the jump down from yesterday's close (yesterday's close − low).

Thanks to the jumps, gaps between candles count too. ATR is then Wilder's average of the true ranges over
N candles: every day 1/N of the new range is added to it. Go through it:

ATR has price units, so for Bitcoin at 60,000 it comes out differently than at 20,000. In this course's
charts we therefore also show it as a percentage of the price: on 10 June 2024 ATR 14 was only 2.8% of
the price, on 5 August 2024, during the fall to 49,000, already 5.8%. Move the period:

## Supertrend: a handrail made of ATR

The <KeyTerm term="supertrend">Supertrend</KeyTerm> takes the middle of the candle,
(high + low) / 2, and subtracts or adds a multiple of ATR – on Binance **3 × ATR
10**:

- **in a rise** the line lies 3 ATR below the middle of the candle and moves **only up**,
- **in a fall** it lies 3 ATR above it and moves **only down**,
- when the **close** crosses the line, the trend flips and the line jumps to the other side.

Notice that when the market is wild (ATR high), the handrail is further from the price and survives
bigger swings. In a calm market it comes closer and a smaller move is enough.

## On Binance

**Original** mode → indicator icon → **Main Indicator** → **SUPER**. You set the ATR length (ATR Length,
default 10), the multiplier (Factor, default 3), the colours for up and down and the background fill.
ATR itself is not in Binance's menu – it lives inside Supertrend:

/>

A higher Factor gives the handrail more room – fewer flips, a later exit. A lower one pulls it in – more
flips.

<Callout type="warning" title="Supertrend flips only after the move">
  Supertrend flips only when the close crosses a line several ATRs away. That
  means an exit based on it comes only after a fall of several "normal" daily
  moves. In a sideways market it flips often, and late every time.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"A green Supertrend = the price will rise."** Green only means the line below the price has not been crossed yet.
- **"ATR shows the direction."** ATR measures the size of a move, not its direction – it rises in a crash and in a rocket rally alike.
- **"Supertrend is never wrong because it never backs down."** It does not back down, but it can flip – and in a chop it flips often.

<Callout title="Summary">

- True range = the largest of (high − low, jump up, jump down); ATR is its Wilder average.
- Supertrend = middle of the candle ∓ 3 × ATR 10; it moves only with the trend.
- It flips when the close crosses the line.
- On Binance: Original → indicator icon → Main Indicator → SUPER (10, 3).

</Callout>
