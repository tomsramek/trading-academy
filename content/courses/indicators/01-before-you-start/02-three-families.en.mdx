If indicators held a family reunion, they would sit at three tables. At the first sit those who follow
the direction. At the second those who measure speed and keep fidgeting. And at the third those who
count people. Almost every indicator in the menu has a seat at one of them.

<Illustration name="threeFamilies" />

## Trend: where it is going

Trend indicators have the same units as the price, so they are drawn **over the candles** – on Binance
under the **Main Indicator** tab. They include averages (MA, EMA, WMA), Bollinger Bands (BOLL), VWAP,
Parabolic SAR and Supertrend. They answer: **which way is the market moving and where is the "normal"
price?**

## Oscillators: how fast

An <KeyTerm term="oscillator">oscillator</KeyTerm> swings within a range, often 0 to 100, and has its
own panel **below the chart** (the **Sub Indicator** tab). It includes RSI, StochRSI, KDJ, Williams %R,
CCI and MACD. They answer: **how fast and how "stretched" is the move?**

## Volume: how many people

Volume indicators count how much was traded: VOL, OBV and MFI (which is half an oscillator). They
answer: **is there a crowd behind the move, or a few random trades?**

## Same family = same answer

RSI, StochRSI, KDJ and Williams %R compute almost the same thing – just with a different equation. When
all four show "overbought", you do not have four confirmations. You have one piece of information four
times. It is like asking four people the way when they all look at the same map.

A real second opinion only comes from an indicator of **another** family: trend asks about direction,
an oscillator about speed, volume about participation.

<Callout type="warning" title="More lines ≠ more certainty">
  Every extra indicator adds another chance of a contradiction – and with it the
  temptation to pick the one that says what you want to hear. Fewer lines are
  easier and more honest to read.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"When RSI and KDJ say the same thing, it is certain."** They measure the same thing, so they almost always agree.
- **"MACD is a trend indicator, so it belongs over the candles."** MACD follows averages, but it is drawn in a panel like an oscillator.
- **"Volume is boring."** Volume is the only common indicator that does not stand on the price alone.

<Callout title="Summary">

- Trend (over the candles): MA, EMA, WMA, BOLL, VWAP, SAR, Supertrend – where it is going.
- Oscillators (panel below the chart): RSI, StochRSI, KDJ, Williams %R, CCI, MACD – how fast.
- Volume: VOL, OBV, MFI – how many people take part.
- Two indicators from the same family are not two independent confirmations.

</Callout>
