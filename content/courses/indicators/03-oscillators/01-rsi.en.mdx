The <KeyTerm term="rsi">RSI</KeyTerm> indicator is the market's thermometer: it shows how
"heated" a move is on a scale from 0 to 100. Above 70 people speak of
overbought, below 30 of oversold. Professor Wick starts sweating at seventy –
but nobody ever sold anything to a thermometer.

<Illustration name="rsiThermometer" />

## How RSI is computed

RSI compares how much the price on average **rose** and how much it **fell** over the last N candles:

1. For each candle compute the change against the previous close. A rise goes to "gains", a fall to
   "losses".
2. Average the gains and losses over the last N candles (default N = 14). Later values only move 1/N of
   the way towards the new change – so-called Wilder smoothing.
3. The ratio of the average gain to the average loss is RS. From it RSI = 100 − 100 / (1 + RS).

When the market only rises, losses are zero and RSI is 100. When rises and falls are equal, RSI is 50.

## Where to find it on Binance

In **Original** mode click the indicator icon above the chart, switch to the **Sub Indicator** tab, tick
**RSI** and click **Save**. A panel with **three** lines appears below the chart: RSI(6), RSI(12) and
RSI(24), with dashed levels at 70 and 30. The short RSI 6 is very sensitive, the long RSI 24 calm. In
**Trading View** mode and in most textbooks a single RSI 14 is used – the one the charts in this course
draw.

## Overbought does not mean "sell"

Bitcoin's RSI 14 crossed 70 on 9 February 2024 (close 47,133). Anyone who sold then because "it is
overbought" watched RSI cross seventy again on 26 February and 6 March, and the price closed at 73,072 on
13 March. Over the whole period shown, RSI was above 70 on 84 days out of 444.

In a strong trend, "overbought" is more a sign of strength than of weakness. RSI says the most in a
market moving sideways – and above all together with other tools. More on working with it is in the
lesson [RSI and momentum](/courses/trading-with-a-plan/rsi-and-momentum).

<Callout type="warning" title="RSI is not a trade signal">
  The 70 and 30 levels are not "sell" and "buy" orders. An overbought market can
  keep rising and an oversold one keep falling. Trading on RSI alone without a
  stop-loss and a plan is a bet, not a strategy.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"RSI above 70 = sell."** In a strong rise RSI often stays above 70 for weeks.
- **"Three RSIs on Binance are three signals."** It is one measure with three periods – one piece of information three times.
- **"RSI 50 is neutral, so it says nothing."** It says rises and falls are balanced – neither side has the upper hand.

<Callout title="Summary">

- RSI = 100 − 100 / (1 + RS), where RS is the average rise divided by the average fall over N candles.
- Binance's Original mode draws RSI 6, 12 and 24; the common standard is RSI 14.
- A shorter period = more entries into the 70/30 zones, more false signals.
- Overbought and oversold describe a state, they are not trading orders.

</Callout>
