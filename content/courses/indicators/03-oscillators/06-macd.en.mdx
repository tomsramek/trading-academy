Two trains run in the same direction: a fast one and a slow one. When the fast one pulls away, the gap
grows – the trend is speeding up. When the slow one catches up, the gap shrinks. <KeyTerm term="macd">MACD</KeyTerm> measures exactly that gap. Professor Wick stands on the platform and
announces: "Mind the gap."

<Illustration name="twoTrains" />

## How it is computed

MACD stands on two <KeyTerm term="ema">exponential moving averages</KeyTerm> of the closing price:

1. **MACD** = EMA 12 − EMA 26. A positive number: the fast average is above the slow one, the market has
   sped up upwards recently.
2. **Signal line** = EMA 9 of the MACD values – a smoothed version of the first line.
3. **Histogram** = MACD − signal line. The bars show whether the gap is growing or shrinking.

That is why MACD is drawn in a panel like an oscillator, even though it is made from averages. Walk
through it:

## Where to find it on Binance

In **Original** mode: the indicator icon, the **Sub Indicator** tab, tick **MACD**, **Save**. The legend
shows **MACD(12, 26, 9)**, and both lines with the histogram appear below the chart.

## Crosses: signal or noise?

The best-known use: MACD crosses the signal line from below (bullish) or from above (bearish). On
12 July 2024 MACD rose above the signal line with a close of 57,889, and on 29 July the price was at
66,785. Nice. But over the period shown – about 13 months – MACD crossed the signal line **28 times**.
Some crosses came within a day or two of each other (11 and 12 May, 26 and 28 October).

A cross always comes **after** the move – MACD is built from averages, and they lag. In a market without
a trend the lines intertwine and every cross is just more noise.

<Callout type="warning" title="28 crosses are not 28 trades">
  Anyone trading every MACD cross without another filter would pay fees and
  slippage for dozens of false signals. A cross is information about a change of
  pace, not an order to buy or sell.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"MACD predicts a reversal."** It confirms one – and late, because it stands on averages.
- **"MACD above zero = buy."** It only means EMA 12 is above EMA 26.
- **"The histogram is volume."** The histogram is the difference of MACD and the signal line; it has nothing to do with volume.

<Callout title="Summary">

- MACD = EMA 12 − EMA 26; signal line = EMA 9 of MACD; histogram = their difference.
- On Binance MACD(12, 26, 9) is under the Sub Indicator tab.
- A cross with the signal line shows a change of pace – always late.
- 28 crosses in 13 months: without context most of them are noise.
