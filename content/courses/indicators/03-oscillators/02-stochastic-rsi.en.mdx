When RSI drinks two espressos, you get <KeyTerm term="stochastic-rsi">Stochastic RSI</KeyTerm>. It
brings no new information about the market – it just takes RSI and shows where it sits within its own
recent range. The result is fast, sensitive and restless. Professor Wick recommends reading it sitting
down.

<Illustration name="doubleEspresso" />

## How it is computed

StochRSI takes the RSI values and applies the stochastic oscillator's formula to them:

1. Compute RSI (on Binance RSI 14).
2. Find the lowest and highest RSI of the last 14 candles.
3. Find where today's RSI sits in that range: (RSI − low) / (high − low) × 100.
4. Smooth the result with an average of 3 candles – that is the **%K** line. An average of 3 %K values
   is the **%D** line.

An example: over the last 14 days RSI was at most 70 and at least 40. Today it is 64. StochRSI is
(64 − 40) / (70 − 40) × 100 = **80**. RSI 64 would excite nobody – StochRSI 80 already reports "top of
the range".

## Where to find it on Binance

In **Original** mode: the indicator icon above the chart, the **Sub Indicator** tab, tick **StochRSI**,
**Save**. The legend shows **StochRSI(14, 14, 3, 3)** – the RSI length, the stochastic length, the %K
smoothing and the %D smoothing.

Forty alarms a year are not forty opportunities. Most are just small wobbles of RSI that StochRSI blows
up to the full scale. It is more useful as a quick timing gauge on short timeframes – and only where
another tool already gives you a reason to trade.

<Callout type="warning" title="Sensitive does not mean accurate">
  StochRSI amplifies noise. It reaches 80 or 20 on a quite ordinary day. Anyone
  who opens trades on it without another filter mostly pays fees and slippage.
</Callout>

## Common myths

- **"StochRSI is a better RSI."** It is RSI stretched to the full scale – faster, not smarter.
- **"StochRSI 100 = the price is at the top."** It only means RSI is at the high of the last 14 candles.
- **"When %K and %D cross, it is a signal."** They cross so often that without context it is closer to a coin toss.

<Callout title="Summary">

- StochRSI = (RSI − lowest RSI) / (highest RSI − lowest RSI) × 100 over the last N candles.
- On Binance StochRSI(14, 14, 3, 3): RSI length, stochastic length, %K and %D smoothing.
- It swings far more than RSI and blows up small wobbles.
- It is useful for timing at most, not as a reason to trade on its own.

</Callout>
