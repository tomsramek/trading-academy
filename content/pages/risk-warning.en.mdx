The content of Trading Academy is educational and **is not investment advice**. These are the most
important risks you should know before you put any money into crypto.

## You can lose everything

Crypto prices are extremely volatile. Drops of tens of percent within a few days are not unusual, and
many projects disappear completely – their tokens are then worth nothing. Only invest money you can
afford to lose. Never invest money you need to live on, and never invest on credit.

## Leverage and derivatives

Trading with leverage (CFDs, futures, margin trading) magnifies both profits and losses. You can lose
your whole deposit within minutes. According to the EU's ESMA, 74–89% of retail investor accounts lose
money trading CFDs.

## No protection like at a bank

Crypto-assets are not covered by deposit insurance like money in a bank. The EU's MiCA regulation sets
requirements for crypto-asset service providers, but it **does not protect you from falling prices**.
An exchange can go bankrupt or be hacked – and the money on it can be frozen or disappear. Outside
the EU, different and often weaker rules apply.

## Transactions cannot be reversed

Nobody can cancel a payment on a blockchain. A typo in an address, a lost seed phrase or a scam means
the money is gone. Scammers often pose as exchanges, celebrities or "investment advisers" and promise a
sure profit – such a promise is always a warning sign.

## Taxes

Profits from crypto can be taxable. The rules differ by country and change over time. You are
responsible for paying the right taxes – if in doubt, ask a tax adviser.

## The past does not guarantee the future

The charts and examples in the lessons show what happened in the past. None of it guarantees how the
price will move next.
