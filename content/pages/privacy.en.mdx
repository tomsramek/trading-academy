You can read the Trading Academy courses without signing up and without giving any personal data. We
process personal data only when you create an account. Here is what it is and what we do with it.

## Who processes the data

The data controller is Tom Sramek, who runs trading-academy.app. Contact:
[tom@tomsramek.com](mailto:tom@tomsramek.com).

## Without an account

When you only read the courses, we have no data about you. The site uses no analytics, no ads and no
tracking tools, and it sets no cookies.

The server may briefly record technical details of requests, such as the IP address and time, for
security and troubleshooting.

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is stored only in your browser (local
storage). It never reaches us.

## With an account

When you create an account, we process:

- **your e-mail address** – to send you a sign-in link and to recognise you,
- **your name and profile picture** – only if you sign in with Google and Google passes them on,
- **your Google account identifier** – if you sign in with Google,
- **sign-in details** – when you signed in, the IP address and browser type of each signed-in
  device, to protect the account from misuse,
- **your course progress** – which lessons you completed and when, and your quiz results (best
  and last score, number of attempts). Badges are calculated from it and not stored separately.

We do not use or store passwords. You sign in with a link from an e-mail or with Google.

## Why, and on what legal basis

- **Running your account** – to provide the account you asked for and save your progress
  (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Backups** – so that a server failure does not wipe out your account and progress (legitimate
  interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Security** – limiting sign-in attempts and protecting accounts from misuse (legitimate
  interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not sell the data, use it for advertising or for automated decision-making.

## Cookies

After you sign in we set a single cookie: `better-auth.session_token`. It keeps you signed in for 7
days and is strictly necessary for the account, so it needs no consent and the site has no cookie
banner. Scripts on the page cannot read it (HttpOnly). It disappears when you sign out.

## Who else has access

We work with a few providers who process the data only on our behalf:

| Provider                        | What for                                 | Where    |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Hetzner Online GmbH             | the server, database and daily backups   | Germany  |
| Cloudflare, Inc.                | an encrypted copy of the daily backups   | EU       |
| Forward Email LLC               | sending the e-mail with the sign-in link | USA      |
| Google (Google Ireland Limited) | signing in with Google, if you choose it | EU / USA |

When you sign in with Google, Google's side of the processing follows its
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Donations

If you decide to support the academy, the payment happens on the Buy Me a Coffee website (Buy Me a
Coffee, Inc., USA) and follows their [privacy policy](https://buymeacoffee.com/privacy-policy). Our site
only has a plain link – no script or cookies of theirs. About a donation we see only what Buy Me a
Coffee shows us: the name or nickname you enter, the amount, an optional message and your e-mail. We
do not link this to your academy account and never publish it.

## For how long

- **Your account and progress** are kept until you delete the account. You can delete it yourself
  on the My account page, or write to us.
- **Sign-in details** are deleted when you sign out. Expired records (after 7 days) are removed
  regularly.
- **Sign-in links** work for 15 minutes and are deleted once used or expired.
- **Backups** are kept for 30 days. After you delete your account, your data may remain in older
  backups for up to 30 days, until they are deleted. We use backups only to restore the site after
  a failure.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing
and to data portability (an export), and the right to object to processing based on legitimate
interest. Just write to [tom@tomsramek.com](mailto:tom@tomsramek.com) – we reply within a month.

If you believe we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Czech data protection authority,
[Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů](https://uoou.gov.cz), or to the authority in your country.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the way the site works changes. The current version is always on this
page with the date of the last update.
