Every Trading Academy course is free and will stay free. Nothing is locked, there are no ads, we do not
track you and we do not sell "secret signals". Running the academy does cost something, though – and
Professor Wick has been staring at charts all night, so he could really use a coffee.

## What your support pays for

- **Running costs** – the server, database, backups, domain and the sign-in e-mails.
- **New courses** – time to write, draw charts and record walkthroughs.
- **Updates** – crypto changes fast, so even finished lessons need regular fixes.

## What a donation does not change

- **It unlocks nothing.** Every lesson, quiz and badge is the same for everyone.
- **Nobody sees who donated.** We do not list donors anywhere on the site or treat them differently.
- **It is not an investment or a subscription.** It is a one-off thank you – or a monthly one, if
  you like.

## How to donate

We accept donations through Buy Me a Coffee. The payment happens on their website and follows their
terms. They have no script or cookies on our site. What we see about a donation and what we do with it
is in the [privacy policy](/privacy#donations).

Cannot or do not want to give money? It helps just as much to recommend the academy to a friend, or to
write to [tom@tomsramek.com](mailto:tom@tomsramek.com) when you find a mistake.
